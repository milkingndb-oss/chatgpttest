# Location Bible

Use this bible to keep image generation visually consistent across scenes. The world blends modern academies, system interfaces, cultivation-style power ranks, military bases, and enormous monster battlefields.

## Global Visual Rules

- **Technology language**: smart wristbands, water-screen projections, holographic maps, translucent blue-black stat cards, clean white system text.
- **Magic language**: summoning circles, soul-link threads, rank seals, sacred gold light, black devouring flame, crimson moonlight for Diana's battle forms.
- **Architecture language**: modern Chinese-inspired academy campuses, fortified military infrastructure, bronze dungeon gates, stone training halls, dense mountain wilderness.
- **Monster scale**: low-level monsters are human to truck sized; colossal beasts should feel like moving buildings, ridges, or mountain sections.
- **Continuity colors**:
  - Chenlin and system UI: cool blue-white highlights.
  - Hey Ming: black fur, black flame, gray deathly eyes, expanding shadow/phantom aura.
  - Silver Butterfly: polished silver, scarlet wing accents, metallic red weapon arcs.
  - Diana: sacred gold in child/support form; silver, crimson, and dark red in moon combat form.
  - Alien/Fallen forces: gray-black, ash violet, sickly green, corrupted red.
